What is this voucher?

Because your lifetime spend with PVR crossed ₹10,000, you have been gifted a voucher worth 4 free tickets — any movie, any seat tier, any price range.

How it works

  1. Pick any movie, city, theatre and showtime.
  2. On the seat-selection page, type your voucher code in the “Have a voucher code?” box and click Apply.
  3. Select up to your remaining free tickets and fill in the attendee details.
  4. Confirm — the booking is confirmed instantly, no payment gateway is opened.
  5. Your remaining free tickets update automatically in this marquee and in the voucher box.

Validity

Your voucher is valid for 30 days from the day it was issued — usable right up to the 30th day at 11:59 PM, and invalid from the 31st day at 12:00 AM.

Who can use it, and where

  • Only the account it was issued to — the code is mapped to your account and cannot be shared or transferred.
  • Only on this PVR Cinemas website/app, while signed in — not at ticket counters or other platforms.
  • On any show at any PVR theatre listed on this platform, including premium and recliner seats.

Conditions

  • Worth 4 free tickets in total; each booking can use up to your remaining balance.
  • If you try to book more tickets than your balance, you'll be asked to reduce the count.
  • Unused tickets lapse when the voucher expires — the balance is not carried forward.
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash or credit and cannot be combined with another voucher.
  • If you cancel a voucher booking, the free tickets are returned to your balance while the voucher is still valid.
